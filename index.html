My Journey

Hi, I'm Vincent, a junior front end developer from Indonesia 🇮🇩. I graduated with a degree in Management, a field that seems far from lines of code. Toward the end of my studies, I was finishing my bachelor thesis while helping with my family's business. In between, I tried building my first website, and I fell for it right away. Watching something that only existed in my head come to life on a screen gave me a kind of satisfaction I had never felt before.

From that moment, I knew web development was the direction I wanted to take. After graduating, I've been working with my family's business while focusing on learning web development. I manage my time with discipline so both keep moving forward, and I'm enjoying every step of the process.

Right now, I'm looking for an internship position where I can learn from a real team and contribute to real projects. My skills are HTML and CSS, backed by the Responsive Web Design certification from freeCodeCamp. It is not a quick badge, freeCodeCamp estimates it at about 300 hours of work, and for me it took around 2 months to complete. The learning combines theory, hands-on practice, and quizzes. To earn the certification, I had to complete the required practice and then pass a final exam.

I'm now working toward the freeCodeCamp JavaScript certification. My goal is to become a full stack developer, and this is just the beginning.
